OUR CLUB FACILITIES

Explore what Sterling Sportsmen Association offers to our active members.

Clubhouse

Member Event Reservations

Our spacious clubhouse hosts monthly membership meetings, raffle ticket drawings, and community gatherings.

Member Perk: Active club members can reserve and use the clubhouse for personal events, family gatherings, and special occasions. Contact the board to check date availability and sign up!

Indoor Archery Range

24/7 Member Access

Shoot year-round regardless of Michigan weather! Our indoor target range features well-maintained backstops and dedicated shooting lanes ideal for practicing target skills, tuning bows, and league shoots.

Access: Active members enjoy 24/7 key access to the indoor archery facility.

Outdoor Archery Range

Seasonal Outdoor Targets

Enjoy outdoor practice during the spring, summer, and fall months. The outdoor range provides varied target distances to prepare archers for field competition and bowhunting seasons.

Rifle River Property

River Access & Recreation

Located along the beautiful Rifle River, this private club site is a scenic space for members and their families to enjoy nature.

Activities: Launch a canoe or kayak, spend an afternoon fishing, or bring the family out to relax alongside the water.

Clubhouse Rules & Regulations

All members and guests are expected to adhere to the posted clubhouse rules to ensure safety, cleanliness, and respect for club property.

  • Respect the Grounds: Clean up after all personal events, meetings, or practice sessions.
  • Guest Policy: Members are responsible for the conduct and safety of their accompanied guests.
  • Security: Ensure all doors and entrances are securely locked when leaving the facility.
  • Safety First: Observe all posted range safety rules and firearm/bow safety protocols at all times.
