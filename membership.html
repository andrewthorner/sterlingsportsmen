JOIN THE ASSOCIATION

Become a member of Sterling Sportsmen Association and support local outdoor traditions.

Member Benefits

We welcome sportsmen and sportswomen interested in outdoor conservation, indoor archery, and community fundraising events.

  • Clubhouse Access: Active members have access to the clubhouse facility for regular meetings and member gatherings.
  • Indoor Archery Range: Indoor target shooting range privileges 24/7!
  • Hunter Safety Classes: Priority notification and participation in annual hunter education programs.
  • Community Support: Voting rights at member meetings and involvement in annual events like our 30 Gun Raffle.
  • Rifle River Property Access: Members have acces to the Rifle River property.

Club Facilities

Indoor Archery Range

Our indoor archery range offers year-round shooting positions ideal for practicing target skills, tuning equipment, and participating in indoor shoot events.

Clubhouse Grounds

A comfortable gathering point for members, hosting board meetings, seasonal events, and raffle ticket organization.

Rifle River Site

Our spot on the Rifle River. Located about 4 miles from the club house. This spot is for our members to enjoy everything the river has to offer! Bring you fishing gear, a Kayak, or the family out for a picnic.
