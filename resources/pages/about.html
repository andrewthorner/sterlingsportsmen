SPORTSMEN PLEDGE

"I GIVE MY PLEDGE AS AN AMERICAN TO SAVE AND FAITHFULLY DEFEND FROM WASTE - THE NATURAL RESOURCES OF MY COUNTRY, - IT'S AIR, SOIL AND MINERALS, - IT'S FOREST, WATERS AND WILDLIFE."

Our Mission & Community Purpose

The Sterling Sportsmen Association is a non-profit organization dedicated to preserving Michigan's outdoor heritage, promoting responsible marksmanship, and fostering wildlife conservation.

Our club actively serves the Arenac County community by sponsoring local events, offering state-certified Hunter Safety Education classes for youth and prospective hunters, and maintaining quality facility grounds for local sportsmen and sportswomen.
